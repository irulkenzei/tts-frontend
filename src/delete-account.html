Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Delete your account on the web

If you created your Narator AI account through our website, log in and go to your Account page — there's a "Delete Account" button in the Danger Zone section that permanently deletes your account and data immediately.

Delete your account on mobile

If you're using the Narator AI mobile app, you can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app, in just a few steps:

  1. Open the Narator AI app and make sure you're signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner to open your Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account will be deleted immediately. If you signed up with email/password, Google, Apple, or Facebook, deleting your account also removes access via all of those sign-in methods.

What data is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes: your account profile (name, email), your generated content library (audio files, translations, subtitles), your voice cloning models, and your subscription/quota history associated with your account.

What data may be retained

Some information may be retained for a limited period after account deletion where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes — for example, transaction records related to a purchase, which are kept in accordance with applicable tax and accounting regulations. This information is not used for any other purpose and is deleted once it is no longer required to be kept.

Deleting specific data without deleting your account

You can delete individual items from your Library (generated audio, translations, subtitles) or remove individual cloned voices at any time from within the app, without deleting your entire account.

Need help?

If you're unable to access the app to delete your account, or have questions about this process, contact us at support@naratorai.app and we'll process your deletion request manually.
