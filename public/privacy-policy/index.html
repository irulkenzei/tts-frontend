Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect: (a) voice recordings you submit for cloning; (b) text scripts, documents (EPUB/DOCX), and video/audio files you submit for narration, translation, transcription, or subtitle generation; (c) messages you send to the in-app AI Assistant feature; (d) an anonymous session and device identifier, created automatically when you first open the app, used to save your preferences and generated content across sessions and to enforce free-tier usage limits (e.g. free voice generations, translations, and voice clones); (e) an email address, provided when you subscribe to Narator Pro, used to process and verify your subscription; (f) purchase and subscription history managed by Apple's App Store, Google Play, or — for web purchases — our payment processors Lemon Squeezy (international customers) or Midtrans (customers in Indonesia); (g) push notification tokens, if you enable notifications; (h) app usage analytics collected anonymously; (i) for web purchases only, your approximate country (derived from IP address) used solely to route you to the correct regional payment provider — we do not store precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Voice recordings, scripts, documents, and media files are processed solely to generate the narration, translation, transcription, subtitles, or AI Assistant response you requested. Account, session, and device data are used to manage your session, enforce fair-use limits, and verify subscription status. Email addresses collected at purchase are used only to process and verify that specific transaction and grant Pro access. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The product relies on third-party providers to deliver its features: Appwrite (backend infrastructure for authentication, database storage, and serverless functions), Replicate (voice synthesis, voice cloning, translation, and audio transcription), and, for the AI Assistant feature, one of several AI providers you may select — Google (Gemini), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), or Alibaba Cloud (Qwen). Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store and Google Play (in-app subscriptions), or by Lemon Squeezy and Midtrans (web subscriptions, depending on your region) — we never receive or store your payment card details.

4. Data Retention

We maintain a backend account database to store your account/session details, subscription/quota status, and job history (such as generated audio, translations, and subtitle transcripts) so you can access your generations across sessions. You can request deletion of your account and associated backend data at any time (see Section 7).

5. Data Security

All communications between the app and our backend and third-party providers use HTTPS/TLS encryption.

6. Children's Privacy

Narator AI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You may delete your account and associated data at any time from the Profile/Settings section of the app, or by contacting us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@naratorai.app.
