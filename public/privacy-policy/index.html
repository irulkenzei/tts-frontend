Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect: (a) voice recordings you submit for cloning; (b) text scripts, documents (EPUB/DOCX), and video/audio files you submit for narration, transcription, or subtitle generation; (c) messages you send to the in-app AI Assistant feature; (d) account information (email address, display name, profile photo) if you create an account; (e) device and usage data used to enforce free-tier limits and subscription status; (f) push notification tokens, if you enable notifications; (g) app usage analytics collected anonymously; (h) purchase and subscription history managed by Apple's App Store or Google Play.

2. How We Use Your Information

Voice recordings, scripts, documents, and media files are processed solely to generate the narration, transcription, subtitles, or AI Assistant response you requested. Account and device data are used to manage your session, enforce fair-use limits, and verify subscription status. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Usage analytics help us improve the product.

3. Third-Party Services

The product relies on third-party providers to deliver its features: Replicate (voice synthesis, voice cloning, and audio transcription), and, for the AI Assistant feature, one of several AI providers you may select — Google (Gemini), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), or Alibaba Cloud (Qwen). Your submitted content is processed on these providers' servers under their own privacy and data retention policies. Apple's App Store and Google Play handle all payment processing; we never receive your payment card details.

4. Data Retention

Voice recordings, scripts, and media submitted for processing are retained by the relevant third-party provider according to their own policy. We maintain a backend account database to store your account details, subscription/quota status, and job history (such as subtitle transcripts) so you can access your generations across sessions. You can request deletion of your account and associated backend data at any time (see Section 7).

5. Data Security

All communications between the app and our backend and third-party providers use HTTPS/TLS encryption. Account credentials are managed through our backend authentication provider and are never stored in plain text.

6. Children's Privacy

Narator AI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

7. Your Rights

You may delete your account and associated data at any time from the Profile section of the app, or by contacting us. You may also request deletion of any voice, text, or media data submitted through the product by contacting us. Because purchases are managed by Apple or Google, payment data deletion requests should be directed to Apple or Google directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@naratorai.app.
