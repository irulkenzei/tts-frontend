Feature Requests

Tell us what would make the product better.

Good requests are specific. Explain the workflow, the blocker, and the result you want. That makes it much easier to evaluate and prioritize.

What to include

The screen or feature, who it helps, and what the current limitation is.

Best format

One clear paragraph plus a short example is usually enough.
